# LittleWigs v12.1.23 review

**What it is:** LittleWigs is the dungeon boss-mod pack for BigWigs. The zip holds Lua files with timers, alerts and locale strings for dungeons and delves (Midnight, Legion, Classic and others), plus `.toc` files and a changelog. It has no executables or scripts. The extension glob found no `.exe`, `.sh`, `.py`, `.js` or similar files.

**Checks**
- **Prescan hit, `LittleWigs_Legion/CourtOfStars/Trash.lua:497`.** This is the only chat hit. `sendChatMessage` calls `BigWigsLoader.SendChatMessage` with a `[LittleWigs] ...` prefix, only when `IsInGroup()`, and only to PARTY or INSTANCE_CHAT.
  - It has two call sites. At line 532 it announces a Court of Stars spy-hunt clue, and it only runs when the opt-in `spyHelperEnabled` option is on. At line 556 it announces that the player found the spy.
  - The text comes from the addon's own localized strings, so it isn't spam or a scam, and it isn't sent to individual players. This is a known, documented feature of the addon.
- **Hidden code.** A grep across all `.lua` files for `loadstring`, `RunScript`, `load(`, `setfenv` and escaped-byte blobs found nothing.
- **Gold, mail, item destruction and bindings.** The same grep found no `SendMail`, `DeleteCursorItem`, `SendAddonMessage`, `SetBinding`, `CreateMacro`, `EditMacro` or `Logout`. It also found no `http` URLs in the Lua.
- **Addon messages.** The `DBM_AddonMessage` handler at line 513 only receives DBM Court of Stars clue messages and reads them. Nothing is sent.
- **Text aimed at a reviewer or AI.** I found none in the files I checked.

**Coverage:** I read the prescan hit, its surrounding code and its call sites in full. The rest of the roughly 900 boss-mod files were checked with the pattern searches above, not read line by line.

**Concerns:** None.

VERDICT: PASS

## Codex

LittleWigs provides BigWigs dungeon and scenario warnings, timers, sounds, markers, and encounter helpers. The supplied package contains 907 Lua files, 13 addon manifests, and a changelog.

- **Chat—the sole prescan hit:** [CourtOfStars/Trash.lua:497](/tmp/packmule-review-littlewigs-2Pjo91/addon/builds/LittleWigs-v12.1.23/LittleWigs_Legion/CourtOfStars/Trash.lua:497) sends `[LittleWigs]` puzzle clues to `PARTY` or `INSTANCE_CHAT`. Calls at lines 532 and 556 follow clue discovery or finding the spy, with the disclosed `spy_helper` option enabled. Other `Say`, `Yell`, and countdown calls announce encounter mechanics. These are normal group assistance, not advertising, impersonation, or scams.

- **Addon communication:** [CourtOfStars/Trash.lua:676](/tmp/packmule-review-littlewigs-2Pjo91/addon/builds/LittleWigs-v12.1.23/LittleWigs_Legion/CourtOfStars/Trash.lua:676) collects profession icons and skill levels, then calls `self:Sync("professions", profString)` at line 686 to identify who can activate dungeon buffs. Other messages synchronize clues, available buffs, and [Odyn’s encounter start](/tmp/packmule-review-littlewigs-2Pjo91/addon/builds/LittleWigs-v12.1.23/LittleWigs_Legion/HallsOfValor/Odyn.lua:91). Received data is used for these features, not executed as code.

- **Automatic purchase, missed by the prescan:** [Tazavesh/Trash.lua:624](/tmp/packmule-review-littlewigs-2Pjo91/addon/builds/LittleWigs-v12.1.23/LittleWigs_Shadowlands/Tazavesh/Trash.lua:624) checks `vendor_autopurchase`, restricts the NPC to Xy’darid, and calls `BuyMerchantItem(1, 1)` at line 631 for the trading-game item. I found no mail/trade/auction/guild-bank theft, item destruction, macro or binding manipulation, forced logout, string-code execution, obfuscated payloads, phishing, or reviewer-directed instructions.
